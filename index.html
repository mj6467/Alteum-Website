Frequently asked questions

What does Alteum connect?+

Alteum brings together local payments, foreign exchange, virtual accounts and digital asset infrastructure, with a focus on USDT and USDC.

Which currencies are the main focus?+

MXN, COP, USD and EUR, alongside USDT, USDC and broader crypto coverage.

EUR payments support SEPA and SWIFT.

How do we get started?+

Contact info@alteum.io with your business, markets, currencies and payment requirements. Our team will work through the setup with you.

Can we build this into our product?+

Discuss your integration requirements with our team, including the payments, account infrastructure and digital asset capabilities you want to connect.

Does Alteum support virtual accounts?+

Yes. Talk to our team about virtual account issuance for your business or eligible customers, including account structure and collection requirements.

How are pricing and settlement terms confirmed?+

The team confirms the applicable route, fees, conversion and settlement terms for your business and payment flow.

Which blockchain networks are supported?+

Crypto and wallet infrastructure supports Ethereum, TRON and Solana. Supported tokens, deposit addresses and transfer instructions are confirmed for the selected network.
