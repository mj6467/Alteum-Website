1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how Alteumx International, S.A. (“Alteumx International,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), in connection with the AlteumX and Waltrex brands, collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects personal information when you access or use our websites, platforms, applications, APIs, products, or services (collectively, the “Services”).

Alteum is the business name of Alteumx International, S.A. Its products are AlteumX, available through alteum.io, and Waltrex, available through waltrex.io. This Policy applies to the Services offered under both brands, unless a separate privacy notice expressly applies to a particular service. Where another legal entity is responsible for a specific Service or acts as an independent controller, its identity and applicable privacy terms will be disclosed where required by law.

For purposes of this Policy, “you” or “your” refers to any individual whose personal information we process, including website visitors, prospective customers, representatives of business customers, account holders, and authorized users. Where we process personal information about the customers or end users of our business clients through the Services, we do so on behalf of and under the instructions of that business client. In those cases, the business client is responsible for providing its end users with an appropriate privacy notice and obtaining any required consents.

This Policy should be read together with any applicable service terms and cookie notice. Where consent is legally required, we will obtain it before carrying out the relevant processing. The Services are intended for businesses and are not directed at individuals under the age of eighteen (18). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

2. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us and which Services you use. It may include:

a. Identification and contact information

Full legal name, business contact details, email address, telephone number, business address, date of birth where necessary, and information relating to your role or relationship with a business customer.

b. Account and verification information

Account registration and profile details; identity verification information and supporting documents where required; business registration, ownership, and authorized representative information; and information required for applicable customer due diligence, compliance, and risk assessment procedures.

c. Transaction and payment information

Transaction records, payment instructions, amounts, currencies, timestamps, and status information; relevant bank account details, payment identifiers, and beneficiary information; and digital asset wallet addresses, blockchain transaction identifiers, and related transaction data where applicable to the Services.

Payment information may be processed directly by us or by third-party providers involved in delivering the relevant Services.

d. Technical and usage information

IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, website pages visited, access times, referral URLs, interaction data, log files, diagnostic information, and information collected through cookies or similar technologies.

e. Communications and support information

Messages sent by email or through messaging applications such as WhatsApp, inquiries, support requests, feedback, information provided during commercial discussions or account management, and records of communications where necessary for service delivery, security, or legal compliance.

We seek to collect only information that is relevant and necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

3. HOW WE COLLECT PERSONAL INFORMATION

We may collect personal information directly from you when you contact us, complete a form, register an account, or use our Services; from the business or organization you represent; through identity verification, payment processing, compliance, and technology providers; through third-party platforms or integrations that you authorize us to access; automatically through website logs, cookies, and similar technologies; and from public or legally available sources where appropriate and permitted by applicable law.

Where we rely on information provided by another party, we take reasonable steps to use it for legitimate and authorized purposes.

We may process personal information to:

  1. Create, administer, and maintain accounts and user profiles.
  1. Provide, operate, and support our Services under the AlteumX and Waltrex brands.
  1. Process service requests, payment instructions, transactions, and related business operations.
  1. Verify identities, assess business relationships, and conduct applicable due diligence.
  1. Prevent fraud, detect suspicious activity, protect our systems, and manage operational risks.
  1. Communicate with users, respond to inquiries, and provide customer support.
  1. Monitor, analyze, and improve the performance, security, and functionality of our Services.
  1. Maintain business, operational, accounting, and transaction records.
  1. Comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, and lawful requests from competent authorities.
  1. Send service announcements and, where permitted, marketing communications about our products and services.

Depending on applicable law, our legal basis for processing may include your consent, the performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps, compliance with legal obligations, or our legitimate interests, provided those interests do not override your applicable rights.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent subject to applicable legal requirements. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

We may disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients, where necessary and legally permitted:

a. Within Alteum

We may share information internally among the teams responsible for operating, administering, securing, and supporting AlteumX and Waltrex. Such sharing will be limited to legitimate business purposes and subject to applicable privacy requirements.

b. Service providers and business partners

We may share information with providers supporting our technology infrastructure, hosting, analytics, customer support, identity verification, compliance, payment processing, security, and other operational functions. Where necessary to provide a requested service or process a transaction, we may share relevant information with banks, payment processors, liquidity providers, digital asset service providers, blockchain analytics providers, and other participating institutions or counterparties.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, regulation, legal proceedings, or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where disclosure is otherwise legally permitted to protect our rights, users, or operations.

Information may be disclosed in connection with a merger, restructuring, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to applicable confidentiality and data protection requirements.
