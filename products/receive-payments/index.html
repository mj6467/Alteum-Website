Frequently asked questions

Which currencies can my business receive?−

Alteum supports business collections in MXN, COP, USD and EUR. The account arrangement and payment instructions are confirmed for your collection requirements.

Can we settle in USDT or USDC?+

Yes. Collections can settle in fiat, USDT or USDC. Agree the currency or asset, receiving account or wallet, and any applicable network before funds move.

How do we access the service?+

Collections are available through a personal WhatsApp chat, our non-technical platform, and the Waltrex API. Please contact our team for your desired setup.

Is the collections API the same as the Waltrex API?+

Yes. Collections and wallet services use the same Waltrex API. Explore the Waltrex API for more information.

Which payment methods are available?+
  • MXNSPEI payments with CLABE.
  • EURSEPA and SWIFT payments with IBAN.
  • USDDomestic wire, international wire (SWIFT), ACH, RTP and FedNow.
  • COPBre-B payments with linked-account payment keys.
Which blockchain networks are supported?+

Crypto and wallet infrastructure supports Ethereum, TRON and Solana. Supported tokens, deposit addresses and transfer instructions are confirmed for the selected network.
