Frequently asked questions

Do customers need to include a payment reference code?+

No. Virtual account numbers are unique to each customer and currency, allowing your business to identify and organize incoming collections through the account number itself.

How are client crypto deposits organized?+

Each client receives their own crypto deposit address. Client deposits are automatically swept into a master account wallet under your business name, rather than using a pooled client deposit address.

Can we arrange accounts for our customers?+

Yes. Every customer receives their own distinct fiat deposit account number for each currency. Your business account remains a business account; customer collections use their separate account numbers, without requiring reference codes.

Will the account be in our company’s or customer’s name?+

Accounts may be named for your business or arranged under Alteum’s name. Extended KYC allows a customer-named account. Customer deposit account numbers remain unique to each customer and currency regardless of the name displayed on the account.

Can collections settle in fiat or crypto?+

Discuss your fiat or crypto settlement requirements during setup. USDT and USDC are the main stablecoin focus, with other crypto also supported.

What is needed to get started?+

Share whether you collect for your business or customers, the currencies, payer locations and expected activity. Our team will confirm the account arrangement and onboarding information required.

Which payment methods are available?+
  • MXNSPEI payments with CLABE.
  • EURSEPA and SWIFT payments with IBAN.
  • USDDomestic wire, international wire (SWIFT), ACH, RTP and FedNow.
  • COPBre-B payments with linked-account payment keys.
Which blockchain networks are supported?+

Crypto and wallet infrastructure supports Ethereum, TRON and Solana. Supported tokens, deposit addresses and transfer instructions are confirmed for the selected network.

Can accounts be held under Alteum’s name?+

Yes. Alteum-named account arrangements are available for customers seeking additional privacy and protection. Contact our team to confirm the account structure and applicable terms. Each customer’s deposit account number remains distinct regardless of the displayed account name.
