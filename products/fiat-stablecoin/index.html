Frequently asked questions

Are USDT and USDC the only crypto supported?+

USDT and USDC are the main stablecoin focus. Other crypto is also supported. Contact our team with the asset, amount and receiving destination to confirm your conversion.

Can I convert crypto back to fiat?+

Yes. Discuss your funding asset, fiat currency and receiving bank details with the team to confirm the available conversion and settlement arrangement.

How are rates and fees confirmed?+

Confirm the asset pair, amount, exchange rate, fees and intended receiving amount before funding your conversion.

Which network should I use?+

Use only the network and destination provided for your conversion instruction. Confirm the asset, network and address before transferring.

Which payment methods are available?+
  • MXNSPEI payments with CLABE.
  • EURSEPA and SWIFT payments with IBAN.
  • USDDomestic wire, international wire (SWIFT), ACH, RTP and FedNow.
  • COPBre-B payments with linked-account payment keys.
Which blockchain networks are supported?+

Crypto and wallet infrastructure supports Ethereum, TRON and Solana. Supported tokens, deposit addresses and transfer instructions are confirmed for the selected network.
