Frequently asked questions

Does the recipient need a crypto wallet?+

For a local bank payout, the recipient receives the agreed fiat currency. They do not need a crypto wallet for that payout.

What connects the funding and payout?+

Alteum coordinates the funding, currency conversion, settlement and local payout. USDT and USDC are the primary stablecoin focus.

How are fees and delivery times confirmed?+

Share the funding currency, payout currency, amount and payment purpose. Our team will confirm the funding instructions, conversion terms, charges and expected payout window.

Can we arrange recurring business payments?+

Contact our team with your payment frequency, currencies and recipient requirements to discuss the appropriate setup.

Which payment methods are available?+
  • MXNSPEI payments with CLABE.
  • EURSEPA and SWIFT payments with IBAN.
  • USDDomestic wire, international wire (SWIFT), ACH, RTP and FedNow.
  • COPBre-B payments with linked-account payment keys.
Which blockchain networks are supported?+

Crypto and wallet infrastructure supports Ethereum, TRON and Solana. Supported tokens, deposit addresses and transfer instructions are confirmed for the selected network.
